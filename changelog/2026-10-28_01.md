## Why Care?

event-hog worked the floor at the 7x24 Exchange Fall 2026 with Edviro Energy, and it worked. Now anyone can run it, read it, or fork it, and a CTO can check that it does what we say it does.

## What's New?

- **Its own public repo.** [lossless-group/event-hog-mcp](https://github.com/lossless-group/event-hog-mcp), extracted from a private stack with its history kept. Every deployment now sets its own address and ids.
- **The kit ships with the server.** Skills, the event template, and a generic settings example. No team's real settings or people.
- **A README that says what it's for.** Why it exists, what it does before, during and after an event, three ways to run it (no backend, Cloudflare Artifacts, paired with a CRM), and what the server guarantees versus what it only asks the model to do.
- **COLLABORATE.md.** How to fork, where to start a review, and the rule that no real event data goes in this repo.
- **DATA-POLICY.md.** On our deployment, a team's data is stored so they can use it, and never looked at or used commercially. Draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer.
- **A CRM spec.** [Pick a CRM and Go](https://github.com/lossless-group/event-hog-mcp/blob/main/context-v/specs/Pick-a-CRM-and-Go.md): a team picks HubSpot, Twenty, or plain CSV once, and syncs with one preview and one approval.
- **A splash page** at [lossless-group.github.io/event-hog-mcp](https://lossless-group.github.io/event-hog-mcp/), laid out as a conference: a lanyard badge, the program, three passes, and the workspace as a floor plan.
