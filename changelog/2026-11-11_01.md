## Why Care?

A teammate invited into a team was asked which company they work for, though
the team already knew. Nothing exercised every tool the way two real people
would before a server was handed to a client. And eventhog.cc buried how to
connect under a conference metaphor: an agenda with rooms and tracks, tilted
passes, a floor plan and a scrolling ticker.

## What's New?

- **Invited teammates skip the company question.** `get_started` takes their
  company from their team.
- **Possessives read right.** A team named "Lumen Robotics" is "Lumen
  Robotics' team", not "Robotics's".
- **HubSpot exports use E.164 phones**, matching `Contacts__Met.csv`.
- **The event README's file-naming examples** no longer name 7x24 Exchange.
- **`scripts/simulate-users.mjs`** plays a founder who signs up from the
  landing page and a teammate she invites, through setup, every phase and all
  52 tools: a 500-person roster, look-alike and accented names, and both of
  them logging at the same moment. It deletes its test team at the end with a
  home admin key. The timings in the
  [Nov 12 entry](2026-11-12_01.md) (149s to 96s of server time) come from it.
- **eventhog.cc is four parts:** what event-hog is, the setup prompt (moved up
  to second), what it does before, during and after, and where your data
  lives. The footer is one line of links, and the mode toggle says Light, Dark
  and Vibrant.

## Under the Hood

- The setup prompt and the script that makes each visitor's link are
  unchanged.
- The splash's `program` content collection went with the agenda that read it.
- Shipped from Edviro's fork on branches `fix/teammate-onboarding-polish`
  and `splash/plainer-landing-page`; the Worker deploy ran on 2026-11-12.
