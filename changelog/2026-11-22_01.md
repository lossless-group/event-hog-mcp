## Why Care?

Part of the point of writing about an event is visibility with the people
written about, and drafts named organizations without linking them. Speaker
research was meant to find past talks, but on the NAESCO R3 agenda (45
speakers) no video was found for anyone, because nothing made video its own
search: a general search for a name returns LinkedIn and press first.

## What's New?

- **Drafts link the organizations they name.** Blog posts and LinkedIn link
  each organization's own site at first mention, especially speakers'
  organizations, and a speaker's fitting talk when there is one. Tweets tag
  the organization's handle. URLs come from the speaker profile or the
  organization's own site, never built from a name.
- **Never a competitor.** Competitors are named in plain text, unlinked and
  untagged. The agent judges who competes (anyone selling something like ours
  to the same buyers), since a conference is often where we first hear of
  one, names every organization it left unlinked, and notes new competitors
  for the team. `get_config` can carry an optional `competitors` seed list.
- **Video is its own search.** `augment-speakers-panelists` step 5 runs
  `"<Name>" site:youtube.com`, then name plus session topic, then the
  organization's and event's channels. "No video found" is only allowed with
  the queries listed. The fast pass keeps video; an agenda's worth of speakers
  gets a baseline for all and full research, video included, for the ones that
  matter.
- **Speaker profiles capture the organization's website**, for linking.
- **eventhog.cc** says speaker research includes past talks, and that drafts
  link the people and companies in them.

## Under the Hood

- `save_speaker_profile` replies with a nudge when a profile has no videos and
  its `not_found` doesn't mention a video search.
- `save_draft`'s description carries the link-out and competitor rule, so an
  agent that never loaded the skill still sees it.
- `augment-speakers-panelists` is canonical in `lossless-agent-skills`; the
  kit carries a copy.
