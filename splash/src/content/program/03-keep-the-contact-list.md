Log someone from a badge photo, a business card, a voice memo, or two thumb-typed lines. One file per person; every teammate's interactions and observations append to it, so two people who met the same buyer see each other's notes. A database underneath answers "who have we met from this company?" and "who's in Texas?". The CSV is always current.
